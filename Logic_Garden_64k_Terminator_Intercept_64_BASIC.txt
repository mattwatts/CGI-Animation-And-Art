"THE INTERCEPT [HEAVY METAL]."

We are simulating the T-850 Pursuit Algorithm (Minimize Distance, Avoid Collision). The Lesson: Momentum ($p=mv$).

The Setup:

    The Target (The Crane): A 100-Ton Liebherr Mobile Crane (Massive m). It does not swerve. It plows through obstacles. It is the Unstoppable Force.
    The Interceptor (The Harley): A 300kg Softail (Low m, High Agility). It is the Smart Projectile.
    The Environment: A horizontal parallax strip. The road is scrolling at 80 MPH.
    The Physics:
        The Crane destroys obstacles (Kinetic Energy Transfer > Structural Limits).
        The Bike must utilize Vector Addition (Forward Velocity + Lateral Dodge) to survive the debris field created by the Crane.

"THE INTERCEPT [C64 NATIVE]."

I have optimized the T-850 Pursuit Algorithm for the MOS 6510 Processor. This loader constructs a Composite Sprite Engine. The Crane is too massive for a single hardware sprite, so we weld 4 Sprites together (Sprites 4-7) to create the 100-Ton Liebherr chassis. Arnie (Sprite 0) navigates the "Kill Zone" relative to this massive object.

Hardware Specs:

    Sprites: 5 Active (1 Player, 4 Boss Composite).
    Collision: Hardware Register $D01E (Sprite-to-Sprite).
    Physics: Joystick Port 2.

Technical Breakdown

    Sprite Multiplexing (Fake): Instead of complex raster interrupts, we use the C64's Expand X/Y feature (Registers $D017 / $D01D) on the Crane sprites (4-7). This sets the bits to 240 ($F0), making the Crane sprites Double Height and Double Width, effectively creating a massive "Boss" sprite that fills half the screen with minimal data.
    The Crane Geometry:
        Sprite 6: The Chassis (offset Left).
        Sprite 4/5: The Cab and Boom (offset Right/Up).
        Sprite 7: The Rear/Hook.
    Physics: Simple coordinate addition based on $DC00 (CIA#1 Port A) states.
    The Road: Line 1420 uses direct POKES to Screen RAM (1024 + offset). It cycles through the bottom row positions (F AND 31) to create a racing stripe effect moving right-to-left relative to the bike.
